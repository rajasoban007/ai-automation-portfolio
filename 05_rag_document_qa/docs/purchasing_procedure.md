# Purchasing Procedure (Sample Printing Co. — demo document)

## Purchase orders
Every purchase above PKR 50,000 needs a purchase order approved by the procurement manager. Orders above PKR 500,000 also need the managing director's approval.

## Three-way match
An invoice is paid only after it matches the purchase order and the goods received note. Quantity differences above 2% go to the procurement manager for review.

## Urgent orders
Urgent orders may be placed by phone, but the purchase order must be raised within one working day.
