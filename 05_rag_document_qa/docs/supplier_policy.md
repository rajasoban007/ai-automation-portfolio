# Supplier Policy (Sample Printing Co. — demo document)

## Approved suppliers
Paper may only be bought from suppliers on the approved list. A new supplier needs two quotes compared and sign-off from the procurement manager before the first order.

## Payment terms
Standard payment terms are 45 days from the invoice date. Early-payment discounts of 2% are taken when the supplier offers them and cash allows.

## Price changes
Suppliers must give 14 days' written notice of any price increase. Increases above 5% require a renegotiation meeting before new orders are placed.
