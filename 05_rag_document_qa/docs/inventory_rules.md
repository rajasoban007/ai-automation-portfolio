# Inventory Rules (Sample Printing Co. — demo document)

## Stock counts
A full stock count is done at the end of every quarter. High-value items such as CTP plates and ink are cycle-counted every week.

## Reorder levels
Reorder points are reviewed every month using the last 90 days of usage. Safety stock for paper is set to cover two weeks of production.

## Damaged stock
Damaged paper must be recorded on the same day in the damage register and photographed before it is moved.
