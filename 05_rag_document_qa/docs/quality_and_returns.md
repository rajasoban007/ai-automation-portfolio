# Quality and Returns (Sample Printing Co. — demo document)

## Incoming inspection
Every paper delivery is checked for grammage, size and moisture. Ten reams are sampled from each lot.

## Returning goods to suppliers
Defective material must be reported to the supplier within 7 days of delivery. The supplier collects returned goods at its own cost.

## Customer complaints
Customer complaints about print quality are answered within 48 hours, and a root-cause note is filed within 5 working days.
